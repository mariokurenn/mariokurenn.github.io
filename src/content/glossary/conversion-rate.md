**Conversion rate** is the percentage of website visitors who complete a desired action within a given time period. It is the core metric of conversion rate optimization (CRO) and the primary measure of a website's commercial effectiveness.

**Formula:**

> **Conversion Rate = (Conversions ÷ Total Visitors) × 100**

If 1,000 people visit a landing page and 25 of them submit a contact form, the conversion rate is 2.5%.

## What Counts as a Conversion?

A "conversion" is any action you define as valuable. Common conversion types:

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Macro-conversion** | Purchase, paid sign-up, contract signed |
| **Micro-conversion** | Email opt-in, free trial, demo booked |
| **Engagement event** | Video watched, pricing page visited |

Most businesses track one primary macro-conversion as the headline CVR, with micro-conversions monitored separately as leading indicators.

## Why Conversion Rate Matters More Than Traffic

The power of conversion rate improvements is that they compound permanently. A higher CVR applies to every future visitor:

| Scenario | Monthly Visitors | CVR | Monthly Revenue |
|----------|----------------|-----|-----------------|
| Status quo | 10,000 | 2% | $20,000 |
| Double traffic | 20,000 | 2% | $40,000 |
| Double CVR | 10,000 | 4% | $40,000 |

Both approaches deliver the same result — but doubling traffic doubles ad spend. Doubling CVR costs a fraction of that.

## Industry Conversion Rate Benchmarks

- **E-commerce (all products):** 1–4% average · 5–8% top quartile
- **SaaS free trial:** 3–8% average · 15%+ top performers
- **Lead generation landing page:** 5–15% average · 25–35% top performers
- **Email opt-in (content upgrade):** 15–30% average
- **Checkout completion (from cart):** 45–65% average

These figures vary significantly by traffic source — paid traffic typically converts lower than organic; email converts highest of all channels.

## What Affects Conversion Rate?

The five biggest drivers:

1. **Message match** — Does the page reflect exactly what the ad or email promised?
2. **Value proposition clarity** — Can a visitor understand the offer in 5 seconds?
3. **Friction** — How many steps, fields, or decisions stand between visitor and conversion?
4. **Trust signals** — Reviews, case studies, security badges, and social proof reduce risk perception
5. **Traffic quality** — Relevant, intent-matched traffic converts higher regardless of page quality

## How to Improve Conversion Rate

Improving conversion rate requires a structured process — not random changes:

1. **Audit analytics** to identify your highest-traffic, lowest-converting pages
2. **Run qualitative research** — heatmaps, session recordings, user surveys
3. **Form hypotheses** based on observed data, not opinions
4. **A/B test changes** with proper statistical methodology
5. **Implement winners** and build on each test's learnings

This is precisely what a [CRO audit](/services/cro-audit/) systematises — finding the specific issues costing you conversions on your highest-value pages.
