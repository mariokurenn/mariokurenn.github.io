**Trust signals** are elements on a webpage that reduce visitor scepticism and increase confidence in a brand, product, or offer. They address the fundamental question every visitor has before converting: *"Can I trust this?"*

Trust is a prerequisite for conversion. No amount of persuasive copy converts a visitor who doesn't believe you. Research by Edelman's Trust Barometer consistently shows that trust is the top purchase decision factor across product categories — and the first thing prospective customers look for when encountering a brand for the first time.

## Types of Trust Signals

### Social Proof
The most powerful category of trust signals. Reviews, ratings, testimonials, and customer counts all transfer credibility from existing customers to prospective ones.

- **Star ratings with count:** "4.9/5 from 847 reviews" — both components matter
- **Specific named testimonials:** Full name, photo, role, company, and measurable result
- **Customer count:** "Trusted by 12,400 businesses" — specificity signals authenticity
- **Third-party review badges:** G2, Trustpilot, Capterra — signals independent verification

See [Social Proof](/cro-glossary/social-proof/) for detailed implementation.

### Security & Safety
Reduces the perceived risk of sharing information or completing a payment.
- **SSL certificate (HTTPS)** — table stakes; absence actively destroys trust via browser warnings
- **Payment method logos** (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Stripe, Apple Pay) — highest-converting security signals in checkout
- **Third-party security seals** (Norton, McAfee) — lower impact than commonly believed per Baymard Institute research
- **GDPR/privacy compliance notices** — reduces data-sharing anxiety in European markets

### Credentials & Authority
Third-party validation of expertise or quality.
- Industry certifications and accreditations (ISO, professional body memberships)
- Media mentions: "As seen in Forbes / The Guardian / TechCrunch"
- Awards and recognition from recognisable organizations
- Partner or technology integration logos (Google Partner, HubSpot Partner, etc.)

### Guarantees & Risk Reversal
The most controllable trust signal — entirely within your power regardless of review count or history.
- **Money-back guarantee:** removes the financial risk of purchase
- **Free trial with no credit card required:** eliminates upfront commitment
- **"Cancel anytime" for subscriptions:** reduces long-term commitment anxiety
- **Free returns policy for e-commerce:** eliminates product risk for physical goods
- **Satisfaction guarantee:** broader than money-back; covers service quality

A strong guarantee near the CTA consistently produces 10–20% conversion lift because it shifts the risk from buyer to seller — the prospect can commit without fear of losing their money if the product disappoints.

### Transparency
Reduces the "who are these people?" concern — especially important for newer or smaller brands.
- Named team members with real photos (not stock)
- Physical address visible in footer or contact page
- Phone number (signals real company with real accountability)
- Clear, upfront pricing with no "contact for pricing" opacity
- Detailed "about" page with company history and genuine credentials

Transparency signals are disproportionately impactful for B2B buyers, who research companies extensively before purchase and respond strongly to signals of legitimate business operation.

## Trust Signal Conversion Impact

| Trust signal | Typical CVR lift | Best placement |
|---|---|---|
| Specific testimonial (metric + attribution) | 15–34% | Adjacent to CTA, near pricing |
| Money-back guarantee | 10–20% | Adjacent to CTA, checkout |
| Star rating with review count | 12–25% | Hero section, adjacent to CTA |
| Customer count (specific) | 8–15% | Hero section |
| Payment logos | 5–15% | Checkout, near CTA on paid offers |
| Logo bar (recognisable brands) | 5–12% | Below hero, pricing section |
| Security badge | 2–8% | Checkout only |
| "Cancel anytime" copy | 5–12% | Adjacent to subscription CTA |

## Trust Signal Placement

| Location | Best trust signals |
|---|---|
| Hero section | Customer count, star rating, logo bar |
| Adjacent to primary CTA | Star rating, 1-line testimonial, guarantee reminder |
| Pricing section | Money-back guarantee, specific testimonial, review count |
| Checkout step 1 | Payment logos, security indicator, returns policy |
| Checkout step 2 (payment) | SSL indicator, payment logos, guarantee |
| Footer | Certifications, contact info, physical address |

**The CTA proximity rule:** The single highest-impact trust signal placement is directly adjacent to (immediately below or beside) the primary CTA button. Visitors are closest to a decision at that moment — reassurance there resolves final hesitation.

## Trust Signal Mistakes

**Vague signals** — "Industry-leading quality" and "Best-in-class service" are marketing language with zero credibility. Specificity converts: "850+ A/B tests run for 60+ brands" outperforms "extensive experience" by an order of magnitude.

**Mismatched signals** — B2B SaaS buyers care about G2/Capterra category ratings and named enterprise clients. E-commerce buyers care about delivery guarantees and return policies. Mass-market consumer brands need star ratings with high volume. Match signals to what your specific audience actually worries about.

**Below-the-fold only** — Trust signals visible only in the footer or on a dedicated "testimonials page" are seen by 20–30% of visitors. The highest-value trust signals belong in the hero section and adjacent to CTAs.

**Stock photos** — A testimonial photo that looks like stock photography destroys the credibility of the testimonial. Real, slightly imperfect photos convert better than polished stock images because they signal authentic human beings rather than marketing fabrications.

**All signals, no hierarchy** — Cramming every trust signal onto one page creates visual noise and dilutes each signal's impact. Prioritize 2–3 strong signals per section, chosen for their relevance to the conversion decision being made at that point in the page.

## Testing Trust Signals

Trust signals are among the highest-ROI elements to optimize because they address conversion barriers directly:

1. **Type test** — Specific testimonial vs generic testimonial vs no testimonial
2. **Placement test** — Adjacent to CTA vs below hero vs pricing section
3. **Guarantee framing test** — "30-day money-back" vs "Try risk-free for 30 days" vs no guarantee
4. **Review format test** — Star rating badge vs review excerpt vs both
5. **Logo bar test** — Client logos vs media logos vs certification logos
