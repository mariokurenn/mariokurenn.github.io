**Trust signals** are elements on a webpage that reduce visitor scepticism and increase confidence in a brand, product, or offer. They address the fundamental question every visitor has before converting: *"Can I trust this?"*

Trust is a prerequisite for conversion. No amount of persuasive copy converts a visitor who doesn't believe you.

## Types of Trust Signals

### Social Proof
The most powerful trust signals. Reviews, ratings, testimonials, and customer counts all transfer credibility from existing customers to prospective ones.
- Star ratings: "4.9/5 from 847 reviews"
- Specific testimonials with name, photo, role, and measurable result
- Customer count: "Trusted by 12,400 businesses"

### Security & Safety
Reduces the perceived risk of sharing information or making a payment.
- SSL certificate (HTTPS padlock)
- Payment logos (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Stripe)
- Security badges (Norton, McAfee — less impactful than perceived)
- GDPR compliance notice

### Credentials & Authority
Third-party validation of your expertise or quality.
- Industry certifications and accreditations
- Media mentions ("As seen in Forbes")
- Awards and recognition
- Partner or technology logos

### Guarantees & Risk Reversal
Directly removes the fear of loss from purchasing.
- Money-back guarantee
- Free trial with no credit card required
- "Cancel anytime" for subscriptions
- Free returns policy

### Transparency
Reduces the "who are these people?" concern.
- Named team members with photos
- Physical address
- Phone number
- Clear pricing (no "contact for pricing")

## Trust Signal Placement

| Location | Best Trust Signals |
|----------|-------------------|
| Hero section | Customer count, logo bar, star rating |
| Adjacent to CTA | Star rating, guarantee, 1-line testimonial |
| Pricing section | Money-back guarantee, testimonial, logos |
| Checkout | Payment badges, security icon, guarantee |
| Footer | Certifications, contact info, address |

## Trust Signal Mistakes

**Vague signals** — "Industry-leading quality" and "Best-in-class service" are ignored. Specificity converts: "800+ A/B tests run" outperforms "extensive experience."

**Mismatched signals** — B2B SaaS buyers care about G2/Capterra ratings; e-commerce buyers care about delivery guarantees. Match signals to your audience's actual concerns.

**Below-the-fold only** — Trust signals buried at the bottom are seen by 20–30% of visitors. The highest-value trust signals belong adjacent to CTAs and in the hero section.

Trust signal optimisation is one of the most consistently effective areas in [CRO](/services/cro-audit/) — adding the right signal at the right moment can increase conversion rate by 10–30% without changing anything else.
