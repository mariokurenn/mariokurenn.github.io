# Nano Banana Image Prompts — grow-conversions.com Blog

Koristiti ovaj template za svaki blog post. Boja branda: `#1A7F3C` (zelena).
Stil: **clean, minimalist, data-visual, professional**. Bez fotorealizma. Flat design s akcentima u boji branda.

---

## Template za prompt (kopiraj i prilagodi za svaki post)

```
Create a minimalist, flat-design blog hero image for a CRO (conversion rate optimization) website.

Style: Clean, professional, data-focused. Flat design, no gradients, no photos.
Brand color: #1A7F3C (green accent), white background, dark charcoal (#1A1A2E) for text/icons.
Format: 1200x630px, SVG-friendly composition, no text (text will be overlaid in code).

Concept: [INSERT CONCEPT PER ARTICLE BELOW]

Do NOT include: human faces, photographs, shadows, excessive decoration.
DO include: simple geometric shapes, data visualization elements (charts, arrows, graphs), clear visual hierarchy.
```

## Per-Article Prompts

### 1. How to Calculate Conversion Rate
**File:** `/images/blog/how-to-calculate-conversion-rate.webp`
```
Concept: A large, bold mathematical formula in the center: "Conversions ÷ Visitors × 100 = CVR%".
Below the formula, a simple horizontal funnel bar: 10,000 visitors → 250 conversions → 2.5% CVR.
Use green (#1A7F3C) for the result/CVR number. Charcoal for formula elements. White background.
Minimal, clean, educational. The formula should be the visual hero.
```

### 2. How Long Should You Run an A/B Test?
**File:** `/images/blog/how-long-to-run-ab-test.webp`
```
Concept: A horizontal timeline bar divided into two sections.
Left section (red/orange): "Day 4 — 94% confidence" with a stop/warning icon — labeled "Too Early".
Right section (green #1A7F3C): "Day 14+ — 95% confidence + sample size met" — labeled "Valid Result".
Below: two simple line graphs showing CVR fluctuation that stabilizes over time.
Key message: patience = reliable results. Clean, flat, informative.
```

### 3. Conversion Rate Benchmarks by Industry
**File:** `/images/blog/conversion-rate-benchmarks-by-industry.webp`
```
Concept: Horizontal bar chart comparing 6 industries.
Bars from shortest to longest: Travel (1.5%), Electronics (1.2%), B2B Lead Gen (2.5%), SaaS (4%), Finance (5%), Food & Beverage (4%).
Color the highest-performing bar in brand green (#1A7F3C). Others in neutral charcoal tones.
Clean axis labels. Minimal. White background. No legend needed — labels on bars.
Title area (blank — will be added in code): leave top 20% of image empty.
```

### 4. A/B Testing Mistakes
**File:** `/images/blog/ab-testing-mistakes.webp`
```
Concept: A 2×2 grid of minimal icon cards. Each card has a simple warning icon and a label.
Cards: "Stopped too early" / "Too many variables" / "Wrong metric" / "No SRM check"
Use red accents for the warning icons, charcoal for card backgrounds (light grey #F5F5F5).
One card (center-bottom) highlighted in green: "Valid test: defined upfront".
Clean, flat, clear hierarchy. Grid layout. White background.
```

### 5. CRO for Low Traffic
**File:** `/images/blog/cro-low-traffic.webp`
```
Concept: A simple bucket/funnel illustration (flat, geometric, no gradients).
Left: small trickle of traffic flowing into the top (few drops = low traffic).
The bucket has no holes. The output (conversions) at bottom is maximized.
Contrast with a faded ghost image on the right: big flow of traffic into a leaking bucket.
Green (#1A7F3C) highlights the solid bucket. Grey for the leaky ghost.
Message: small traffic, no leaks = efficient conversion.
```

### 6. CRO vs SEO
**File:** `/images/blog/cro-vs-seo.webp`
```

Left column (blue-grey tone): SEO — upward arrow labeled "Traffic Growth", clock icon "3–12 months".
Right column (green #1A7F3C): CRO — percentage symbol "CVR ×2", lightning bolt "4–8 weeks".
Center: a simple equals sign or plus sign — "Both = Revenue Growth".
Bottom: a simple horizontal timeline showing recommended sequence: CRO first → then SEO scales.
Flat, minimal, informative. White background.
```

## WebP Card Images (za blog index)

Za blog index kartice koristiti iste prompte, ali izrenderiraj u **1200×628px** format (Open Graph standard).
Eksportaj kao `.webp` s quality 80. SVG verzija iste kompozicije ostaje za hero na single postu.

---

## Branding Rules za sve slike

- Font u ilustraciji (ako ima teksta): samo sans-serif, bold, charcoal `#1A1A2E`
- Nikad gradijenti, sjene, ili foto-realizam
- Uvijek ostaviti gornji i donji rub čist (title i datum se dodaju u kodu)
- Minimalna veličina SVG: 800×420px
- Svi elementi trebaju biti vidljivi i na tamnom pozadini (za dark mode budući razvoj)
